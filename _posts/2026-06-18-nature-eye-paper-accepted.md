I am delighted to share that our new paper, **"Temporal perceptual training enhances visual acuity in adult amblyopia: a single-case study,"** has been accepted for publication in **Nature Eye**.

This work is part of my PhD thesis and reports an experiment in which we show that **two-flash fusion** may be a potential option for improving visual acuity in adult individuals with amblyopia.

I am very happy to see this work reach publication, and I hope it can contribute to future research on perceptual training and visual rehabilitation.

Many thanks to my supervisor **Luca Battaglini** for his support, guidance, and trust throughout this work.

Paper will be soon added here after production, Read more at: [Nature Eye](https://www.nature.com/eye)
